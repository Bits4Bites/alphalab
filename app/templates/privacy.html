Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 May 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how {{ app_name }} ("the Service") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our platform. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and handling your data transparently.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Authentication data: When you sign in via a third-party provider (e.g., GitHub, LinkedIn), we receive your name, email address, profile picture, and a unique identifier from that provider.
  • Usage data: Information you voluntarily submit during use of the Service, such as ticker symbols, portfolio holdings, investment preferences, and search queries.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not store passwords — authentication is handled entirely by third-party OAuth providers.
  • We do not collect financial account credentials or brokerage login details.
  • We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics services.

4. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and manage access to the Service;
  • Process your requests and generate AI-powered analysis in real-time;
  • Enforce access control via the allowed email list.

5. Data Retention

We do not retain user-submitted data (e.g., ticker queries, portfolio holdings) beyond the duration of the session. AI-generated responses are not stored on our servers. Authentication tokens are stored as browser cookies and expire according to the configured session duration.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • AI providers: Your queries are sent to configured AI service providers (e.g., OpenAI, Google Gemini) for processing. These providers have their own privacy policies governing data handling.
  • OAuth providers: Authentication data is exchanged with your chosen identity provider (GitHub, LinkedIn) as part of the sign-in process.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including:

  • Using HTTPS for all communications;
  • Storing authentication tokens as HTTP-only, secure cookies;
  • Restricting access via email allowlists;
  • Not persisting sensitive user data on our servers.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about the data we hold about you;
  • Request deletion of your data;
  • Withdraw consent for data processing;
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Since we do not retain user-submitted data beyond sessions, most data subject requests are satisfied by default.

9. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • AI providers (e.g., OpenAI, Google, Azure, OpenRouter)
  • Identity providers (e.g., GitHub, LinkedIn)
  • Market data providers (e.g., Yahoo Finance)

We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

12. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact the site administrator.
